PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy at Cosmic Word

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Game data

Cosmic Word does not require an account. Your guesses, game progress, statistics, and display preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. This information is not sent to a Cosmic Word application server. Clearing the browser's site data can remove it.

Hosting and technical logs

During the hosting migration, Cosmic Word may be served from Netlify at astrodle.netlify.app or from Cloudflare Pages at a pages.dev address. The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser/device details, and security logs to deliver, diagnose, and protect the website. Read the Netlify Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Advertising and analytics

The site loads Google's AdSense publisher script for site connection and review, but no production AdSense ad units are configured. Google Publisher Tag test creatives have been removed. Adsterra has not yet been integrated and no live Adsterra ads are being served. When the AdSense publisher script loads, Google may receive technical request information such as IP address, browser/device details, and page information. See the Google Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices. Before enabling Adsterra or any other live advertising provider, this policy must be updated to describe the actual script, its data practices, and any applicable consent requirements.

Children and personal information

Please do not send personal or sensitive information through the game; it has no form for collecting such information.

Contact

For project questions, open the Cosmic Word GitHub repository.
