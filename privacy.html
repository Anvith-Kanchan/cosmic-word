PRIVACY POLICY

Your data stays on your device.

Cosmic Word is a static website. Game progress, guesses, streaks, and preferences are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This project does not currently require an account.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the site. Review Netlify's privacy policy for details.

Advertising and analytics

The visible advertising areas are placeholders and do not currently load an advertising network or collect advertising identifiers through this site. If a real advertising or analytics provider is added later, this policy must be updated to identify the provider, explain relevant data use, and provide consent controls where legally required.

Contact

For questions about this independent project, use the contact information published by the project owner on the linked project repository.
