PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy at Cosmic Word

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Game data

Cosmic Word does not require an account. Your guesses, game progress, statistics, and display preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. This information is not sent to a Cosmic Word application server. Clearing the browser's site data can remove it.

Hosting and technical logs

Cosmic Word is hosted on Cloudflare Workers at cosmic-word.anvian.workers.dev. Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses, requested URLs, browser/device details, and security logs to deliver, diagnose, and protect the website. Read the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Advertising and analytics

Cosmic Word uses Adsterra to serve a 300×250 banner and a native banner ad unit. These units load scripts supplied by the Adsterra publisher dashboard, including scripts hosted at bicea.org. Adsterra and its advertising partners may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, the page URL, and referrer information, and may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, or personalization. Their practices may vary by ad and region. Read Adsterra's Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy for details.

The site also loads Google's AdSense publisher script, but no production AdSense ad units are configured. Google may receive technical request information when that script loads. See the Google Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices.

Children and personal information

Please do not send personal or sensitive information through the game; it has no form for collecting such information.

Contact

For project questions, open the Cosmic Word GitHub repository.
