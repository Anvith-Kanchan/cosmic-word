PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy at Cosmic Word

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Game data

Cosmic Word does not require an account. Your guesses, game progress, statistics, and display preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. This information is not sent to a Cosmic Word application server. Clearing the browser's site data can remove it.

Hosting and technical logs

This website is hosted by Netlify. Like other website hosts, Netlify may process technical data such as IP addresses, request details, and security logs to deliver and protect the website. See the Netlify Privacy Policy for more information.

Advertising and analytics

The homepage loads Google's Google Publisher Tag script and uses Google's documented test ad unit to request generic test creatives. These are development/test ads, not live commercial campaigns, and the site is not configured to earn revenue from them. Google may receive technical request information, such as an IP address, browser/device details, and page information, when delivering its script and test response. See the Google Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices. No other advertising network or analytics service is currently configured. If this site later switches to live advertising or adds analytics, this policy must be updated to explain the applicable providers, data uses, cookies/identifiers, and consent choices before that change.

Children and personal information

Please do not send personal or sensitive information through the game; it has no form for collecting such information.

Contact

For project questions, open the Cosmic Word GitHub repository.
