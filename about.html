ABOUT THE PROJECT

Keep looking up.

Cosmic Word is an independent astronomy-themed daily word puzzle. Each day, players solve a short astronomy clue in six attempts. The answer length varies from puzzle to puzzle, and the grid adjusts to fit.

Why astronomy?

The clue pool draws on planets, stars, missions, physics, celestial coordinates, and discoveries. Answers are complete terms rather than shortened fragments.

Independent project

Cosmic Word is an independent project and is not affiliated with The New York Times or its Wordle game.
