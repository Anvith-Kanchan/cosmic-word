ABOUT THE PROJECT

Keep looking up.

Cosmic Word is an independent astronomy-themed daily word puzzle. Each day, players have six attempts to discover a five-letter word inspired by the universe.

Why astronomy?

Space offers an endless vocabulary of planets, stars, missions, physics, and discoveries. The game is a small invitation to stay curious and learn something new.

Independent project

Cosmic Word is an independent project and is not affiliated with The New York Times or its Wordle game.
